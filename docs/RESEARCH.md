# Hackathon Alignment

## NextStep Hacks 2026

Theme: Earth Forward. The event asks participants to use technology to create meaningful environmental impact across areas including waste reduction, sustainable communities, conservation, renewable energy, climate resilience, and sustainable agriculture.

## Why ReTray fits

ReTray addresses waste reduction at the moment it is created. A successful circulation replaces a disposable takeaway pack with a reusable container that the venue can wash and issue again.

## Judging strategy

- Originality: show packaging operations, not a generic sustainability dashboard.
- Theme adherence: the impact is a direct result of the product action.
- Completion: show the whole loop working live.
- Learning: explain the new operational model simply.
- Design: make the operator and customer experiences precise and different.
- Technology: demonstrate deterministic state changes, not decorative screens.

## Submission constraints

Use a short product video, source repository, and live site where applicable. Keep all product claims exactly within the working prototype.
